# Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, no harassment or personal attacks, keep discussions about the work. Maintainers may edit, remove or lock content that violates this, and may block repeat offenders.

Report problems privately to fortunecodec@gmail.com.
